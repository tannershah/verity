## Notes on source reliability (for the reviewing model)

- Several URLs above were located via `web_search` snippets rather than direct
  `web_fetch` verification of the full page. Before citing any of these in the
  Wharton Studio proposal, re-fetch and confirm: (1) exact page still resolves,
  (2) figures/quotes match the cached snippet, (3) DOI/arXiv ID is correct.
- The NELL "117M beliefs" and some precision figures traced to Grokipedia
  (https://grokipedia.com/page/never_ending_language_learning) are flagged in
  the main survey as **low-reliability secondary source** — prefer the AAAI 2010
  and CACM 2018 primary papers for any NELL numbers used in the proposal.
- FEVER (Thorne 2018) and SciFact (Wadden 2020) canonical URLs were not
  re-verified in this pass (well-known, stable citations) — confirm via
  ACL Anthology / arXiv before final proposal submission if exact page numbers
  or DOIs are needed.
- Science-journal URLs (Costello et al. 2024, the Bago & Bonnefon perspective,
  and the June 2026 Editorial Expression of Concern) require DOI resolution via
  https://doi.org — direct science.org URLs were not captured in this pass.
