# Verity — Epistemic Transparency Engine

*Wharton Generative AI Studio — Tanner Shah, August 2026*

## Problem

Composite claims travel as take-it-or-leave-it units. A reader confronting "X causes Y" cannot see which premises the claim stands on, how strong the evidence under each is, or whether a load-bearing source has been retracted. Nearly 55,000 retractions are machine-readable via Retraction Watch and Crossref as of end-2024, yet no claim downstream of a retracted paper gets flagged. Verdict issuers (PolitiFact, Originality.ai) demand trust transfer that fails exactly on contested claims; transparency tools stop at the article, source, or citation level, leaving the claim itself opaque.

## Gap

Every ingredient ships somewhere today; the conjunction does not. Automatic claim decomposition ships in Loki (open-source) — but into independent atomic facts, ultimately verdict-producing. Study-level evidence metadata ships in scite — supporting and contrasting citations, retraction flags — but per citation statement, no argument layer above it. Symmetric no-verdict presentation ships in Ground News — but at the article and source level, never below. Persistent fact stores ship in Wolfram's curated Knowledgebase and the ClaimReview fact-check caches — but none is dependency-tracked; no shipping product propagates a retraction to the claims depending on it (nearest: RedacTek's paper-level risk scores). The cell where all four meet — entailment-structured premise decomposition, per-premise study-level evidence quality, a dependency-tracked verified-fact store, symmetric no-verdict treatment of contested composites — is empty: none found as of Aug 2026, with targeted existence searches behind each negative claim. The closest research architecture, NELLIE (IJCAI 2024), already does recursive backward-chaining decomposition grounded in a fact store and lacks exactly Verity's three added layers: evidence-quality metadata, invalidation propagation, no-verdict output.

The conjunction is load-bearing because the layers only function together: retraction propagation needs entailment-linked premises to propagate along — atomic-fact decomposition hands a dependency tracker a graph with no edges — and per-premise evidence quality only matters when premises are load-bearing, not independent trivia. That is why NELLIE built a recursive KB-grounded core rather than extending a Loki-style atomic pipeline: a Loki fork reproduces the interface, not the dependency structure the invalidation runs on. Demand for the ingredients is priced — scite and Ground News sustain paid subscription tiers for citation-level evidence metadata and symmetric coverage, respectively. Demand for the conjunction is what the v0 prototype and the roadmap discernment study are designed to test — a build-and-test studio question, not a claimed proven market.

## Approach

Verity backward-chains an input claim into 3–7 load-bearing premises under three validity criteria: joint sufficiency (premises jointly entail the claim, scored by a learned verifier at every step), verifiability descent (each strictly easier to verify), and non-redundancy (removing any one breaks the entailment). Premises are checked against the alethiology, a persistent verified-fact KB with per-fact provenance. Unknown premises trigger agentic retrieval, and every premise carries evidence-quality metadata from free scholarly APIs: retraction status via OpenAlex cross-checked against Crossref, study design via PubMed MeSH, coarse citation intent via Semantic Scholar, sample size text-mined low-confidence or deferred. A justification-based truth-maintenance layer (JTMS, Doyle 1979 — never applied to scholarly retractions; none found as of Aug 2026) propagates any retraction to every dependent claim. Verdicts exist only at the leaves; where evidence conflicts, Verity renders the conflict symmetrically and adjudicates nothing — no root aggregate is ever displayed. Termination is enforced, not assumed — a depth budget plus a per-step verifier gate — and a branch that exhausts verifiability descent surfaces as an unverified premise with its symmetric evidence attached: on contested material, the intended output, not a failure mode. The beachhead is scientific and health claims (news is roadmap), chosen so leaves ground in machine-readable fact — retraction status, study design — rather than contested testimony.

The belief-updating literature motivates this design. Evidence-based AI dialogue can shift beliefs — Costello, Pennycook & Rand, *Science* 2024 (Editorial Expression of Concern, *Science*, June 11, 2026) — a direction corroborated so far only by same-team replications: Boissin et al., *PNAS Nexus* 2025 (three shared authors) and the authors' own unreviewed "Just the facts" preprint. That line is productized today only as persuasion — debunkbot.com argues users out of stated beliefs; none found as of Aug 2026 deploying belief-updating as self-serve symmetric inspection. Verity deliberately does not persuade: belief shifts from AI dialogue build no lasting discernment skills (arXiv:2510.01537), so our human endpoint is discernment and calibration; and the same machinery can persuade people *into* conspiracy beliefs (arXiv:2601.05050) — the verdict boundary's design rationale. Transparency-only presentation has no empirical support yet on either side; measuring it is scoped in Evaluation.

## Evaluation

Established benchmarks first, with comparable numbers: leaf-level premise verification on SciFact (biomedical — the beachhead), FEVER for scale, AVeriTeC for real-world claims; entailment-step validity on EntailmentBank's four-metric suite, with verifier-guided NLProofS the baseline to beat or adopt (Task 2 Overall-AllCorrect 20.9% → 33.3%). Reference bands carry their metric — 2024 and 2025 scores are not comparable: AVeriTeC 2024 top 63% (Hungarian METEOR); 2025 top 0.332 vs. 0.202 baseline (Ev2R). Grounding rate, depth, and budget-exit rate are first-class metrics. Proxy-only components are reported as proxies: decomposition faithfulness via DecompScore and entailment coverage, downstream-verdict sensitivity to decomposition strategy as a first-class number, leave-one-out ablation for non-redundancy, LLM annotation validated by sampled human agreement (the Penn Media Bias Detector template). Where no benchmark exists we build it — both are contributions: (1) contested-claim decomposition, extending AVeriTeC's Conflicting Evidence/Cherry-picking class (<7% of training data, near-zero F1 for most systems) into entailment-tree annotations where the contest localizes to identifiable premises; (2) invalidation propagation — synthetic retraction-injection over the JTMS: retract a leaf; assert every dependent premise flips out and every affected graph re-renders flagged. Roadmap: a discernment/calibration study adapting the belief-updating paradigm to Verity's actual endpoint.
